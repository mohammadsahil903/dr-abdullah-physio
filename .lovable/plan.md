# Dr. Abdullah Physiotherapy Website

## Goal
Build a production-quality, multi-page personal healthcare website that establishes Dr. Abdullah as a trusted physiotherapy professional and makes consultation requests easy on every device.

## Visual direction
- Corporate healthcare aesthetic using off-white, deep navy, and restrained blue/teal accents.
- Plus Jakarta Sans typography, generous spacing, crisp hierarchy, moderate corner rounding, and subtle shadows.
- Authentic treatment-focused photography with a polished editorial treatment; generated visuals will remain clearly illustrative until real approved photographs are supplied.
- Lightweight reveal, hover, accordion, menu, and gallery interactions with reduced-motion support.

## Pages and navigation
- Shared sticky navigation, mobile menu, social placeholders, consultation CTA, responsive footer, and desktop/mobile contact controls.
- Home page with introduction, trust strip, profile, services, differentiators, five-step treatment process, consent-aware patient stories, demo testimonials, gallery preview, featured articles, FAQ, consultation CTA, and contact form.
- Dedicated About, Services, Photos, Blog, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms, and Medical Disclaimer pages.
- Individual article pages using reusable article content, related posts, clear authorship/date/reading-time details, and a medical-information disclaimer.

## Content and safety
- Keep phone, email, location, social links, qualifications, and experience as obvious editable placeholders.
- Mark testimonials and patient stories as demonstration content requiring genuine reviews, permission, and consent before publication.
- Present the service set as editable examples rather than confirmed offerings.
- Avoid unsupported treatment promises, fabricated outcomes, credentials, or statistics.

## Reusable experience
- Create shared site data and components for buttons, section headings, service cards, article cards, testimonials, FAQs, gallery, CTAs, contact forms, and floating contact controls.
- Use accessible landmarks, headings, labels, keyboard states, mobile-friendly touch targets, lazy image loading, and stable image dimensions.
- The contact form will provide a polished front-end submission state; delivery will remain unconnected until real contact details or a message service are supplied.

## SEO
- Add unique titles, descriptions, Open Graph tags, canonical paths, and social card metadata to every content route.
- Add appropriate Physician/MedicalBusiness-style structured data using only safe placeholder facts, plus Article and breadcrumb data on article pages.
- Preserve one clear H1 per page, useful image alt text, and semantic page structure.

## Technical notes
- Implement with TanStack Start and Tailwind CSS tokens in the existing design system.
- Store all editable content in centralized typed data so final details and service lists can be replaced quickly.
- Generate and bundle a cohesive set of professional physiotherapy images; real portraits and patient photographs can replace them later.
- Verify compilation plus desktop and mobile rendering, navigation, menu, FAQs, contact form, and article links.
